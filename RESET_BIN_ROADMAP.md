**Status:** Internal reference. Do NOT push to public repo. Add to .gitignore.

## Phase 1 — Launch Product (Non-smart)
- Premium wardrobe organizer
- Three labeled compartments: Rewear, Wash Soon, Delicates
- Magnetic removable labels
- Soft-close lid
- Scent insert compartment
- Removable inner bags
- Premium wood finish
- Retail price: $120–150

## Phase 2 — Smart Integration (App-connected)

### Auto Wear Tracking
Smart sensors sync with the Outfitted app to automatically log what the user wears. Closet score updates without manual logging.

### Resale Triggers
Intelligent tracking surfaces items the user is no longer reaching for as resale candidates in the P2P marketplace. Closes the loop between physical wardrobe behavior and Outfitted's resale economy.

### Outfit Intelligence
Real-world wear data feeds back into the GPT-4o outfit recommendation engine. Suggestions improve based on what the user *actually* wears, not just what's in their cataloged closet — a defensible data moat the app alone cannot generate.

- The bin is the physical hardware that gives Outfitted a data advantage no software-only competitor can match.
