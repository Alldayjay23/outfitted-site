Shoppable outfits for independent boutiques.

Outfitted brings independent boutiques new customers and helps them sell complete outfits instead of single pieces. Our AI builds outfits from each store's own stock and puts them in front of shoppers at local events, on the shop floor and on the store's website. Checkout stays in the store's own Shopify cart.
