Questions stores ask

The short answers.

What does it cost?

Events need no plan: you pay 12% only on sales we bring you. For your own shoppers, In-store is $199 a month, Online is $299 a month, and both are $399 a month, with no commission on sales from those tools.

Do I have to install anything?

Not for events or in-store. For the Online plan, we add the box to your product pages through Shopify collaborator access, and you can remove it any time.

Who decides which outfits appear?

Outfits publish automatically from your in-stock pieces. You or your stylist can hide any outfit at any time.

What do you keep about my shoppers?

Nothing that identifies them. We count visits and tracked orders, never names, emails or addresses.

I'm not on Shopify. Can I still join?

In-store works from a simple stock sheet. Event pages and the online box use your Shopify cart today. Tell us your setup and we'll tell you what's possible.
