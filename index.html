How it works

Three steps, then it's every morning.

Add your closet

Snap your clothes or pick photos from your camera roll. Outfitted recognizes each piece and sorts it for you.

Get dressed faster

Ask for an outfit for work, a date or the weekend. Every look uses clothes you own, with the weather in mind.

Shop with your closet

Before you buy, see the new piece in outfits with what you already have, so it gets worn instead of returned.
