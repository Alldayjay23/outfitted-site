Internships · Spring 2027

Build the future of fashion with us.

Outfitted AI helps shoppers see how new pieces work with the clothes they already own. We're bringing that experience to boutiques, online stores, and shopping events. Now we're inviting students to help build what comes next.

We're planning our Spring 2027 internship cohort, with an anticipated January start. Explore the areas below and register your interest.

Register Your Interest →
Areas of Interest

AI Styling & Merchandising Intern

Help shape how Outfitted puts outfits together. Review AI-generated looks, research trends and occasion dressing, and curate styling examples using partner-store inventory.

Great for students interested in styling, merchandising, and fashion technology.

Product Design & User Experience Intern

Help make Outfitted easier to use. Test our app and shopping experiences, gather shopper feedback, and turn what you learn into clearer flows and prototypes.

Great for students interested in product design, user research, and technology.

Growth & Community Marketing Intern

Help connect Outfitted with student communities and shoppers. Support campus collaborations, boutique events, and marketing campaigns, then track what gets people involved.

Great for students interested in marketing, community building, and events.

Content & Brand Intern

Bring Outfitted's styling experience to life through short-form video, photography, and social content. Help capture styling demos and create materials for boutique partnerships and events.

Great for students interested in content creation, visual storytelling, and fashion media.

Retail Partnerships & Strategy Intern

Explore how a fashion-tech startup grows through retail partnerships. Research boutiques, prepare outreach materials, support partner launches, and summarize pilot feedback.

Great for students interested in entrepreneurship, business development, and retail.
