Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Outfitted AI LLC ("Outfitted," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Outfitted mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address, from the sign-in method you choose: Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or email and password. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we receive Apple's private relay address instead.
  • Guest Mode: You can use the App without an account. As a guest, your closet list and preferences are stored only on your phone. Photos you add are uploaded to our image host and processed by our AI to recognize the clothes, but they are not linked to an account. If you later create an account or sign in, your guest closet can be moved into it. Deleting the App removes guest data from your phone.
  • Profile Information: Photos you upload of clothing items, style preferences, and occasion information you provide.
  • Closet Data: Information about clothing items you add to your digital closet, including images, categories, tags, and wear history.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, including outfit suggestions viewed, items listed for sale, and retailer products browsed. See Section 3 for product analytics and session replay.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version.
  • Communications: Emails we send about your account, such as email verification and password reset.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Sign you in and keep your account secure
  • Generate personalized AI outfit suggestions from your closet
  • Power virtual try-on features using your uploaded photos
  • Surface personalized retailer product recommendations
  • Enable peer-to-peer marketplace listings and transactions, and process payments and seller payouts
  • Calculate your closet score and usage insights
  • Improve our AI models and app features
  • Communicate with you about your account and app updates

3. Product Analytics and Session Replay

We use PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how the App is used and where it needs to work better. We have used it since June 2026.

  • Analytics events: actions in the App, such as opening it, creating an account, adding a closet item and generating an outfit, along with your device type, operating system and app version. When you are signed in, events are linked to your account's internal ID; when you browse as a guest, to a random ID stored on your device. We do not send your name, email address or phone number to PostHog.
  • Session replay: for a share of sessions, the App may record what appears on screen and where you tap, so we can see where the App is confusing or broken. Before anything leaves your device, we hide (mask) all images, including your closet photos, all text you type, your closet item names, your profile screens and receipt imports. Masked areas are recorded as blank blocks; we cannot see what was in them.
  • How long: session recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • What we don't do: we do not use PostHog data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your account, contact us at Outfittedai@outlook.com.

4. Payments and Shopping Links

  • Payments: Payments are handled by Stripe. When you subscribe or buy an item, your card details go directly to Stripe; we do not see or store your full card number. If you sell items, Stripe collects the information it needs to verify your identity and pay you out.
  • Shopping links: The Shop shows products from eBay and from retailers such as ASOS and Bonheur. When you tap one, it opens on the retailer's website through an affiliate link (the eBay Partner Network for eBay; Awin for ASOS and Bonheur), and we may earn a commission if you buy. We record which product you tapped, linked to your account, so we can count clicks. The link itself does not carry your name, email address or account ID. Once you are on the retailer's website, its own privacy policy and cookies apply, and the affiliate network may record your visit to credit the sale.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors including Firebase (authentication, including Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In), Stripe (payments), Render (hosting and database), Airtable (data storage), Cloudinary (image hosting), OpenAI (AI processing), and PostHog (product analytics and session replay, Section 3) who assist us in operating the App.
  • Marketplace Participants: If you list items for sale, buyers may see your listing information.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at Outfittedai@outlook.com.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The Outfitted App is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at Outfittedai@outlook.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
