About Outfitted

A closet full of clothes, and nothing to wear.

That's where Outfitted started. Most of us own more than enough. What's missing is help putting it together, and knowing whether something new will actually work with the rest.

Our story

From your closet to the store floor.

We built the Outfitted app to turn the clothes you already own into outfits for your day, and to show you how a new piece works with your wardrobe before you buy it.

Shoppers kept asking the same question in stores: "What would I wear this with?" Boutiques hear it at the fitting room every day. So we brought the same styling to independent stores: outfits made only from what's in stock, on the shop floor, on product pages and for local events.

We're a small team in Los Angeles, working directly with shoppers and boutique owners.
